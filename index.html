Survival tips

  • Zombies see poorly at night — but they hear gunshots from far away.
  • Search houses for food, water, meds and weapons.
  • Wood + rag makes bandages. Alcohol + rag makes a molotov.
  • Doors slow zombies down. Windows do not.
  • Keep stamina for the run, not the walk.
